✅ Key Contributions & Strengths

Haytam demonstrates strong self-management skills and consistently meets project deadlines, frequently exceeding expectations when urgent situations arise. He is a key contributor to laboratory operations, playing a critical role in controlling and managing most of the lab machines.

Haytam has played an integral role in supporting and advancing the Wolverine2, Cyclops BU. He is well-acquainted with the DC machine processes and has taken the lead in several processes, including ROSA assembly, TX FAB in Wolverine, and Ewok assembly in Cyclops.

To further strengthen support for Wolverine2 and contribute to continuous improvement efforts, Haytam successfully completed K&S training at FBN. He also took ownership of developing comprehensive and detailed Work Instructions (WIs) for several Wolverine2 processes, ensuring clarity, consistency, and reproducibility.

In addition, Haytam is responsible for creating and developing new DC processes, including defining requirements and ordering new hardware to support these processes. He is currently progressing through SAT MRSI acceptance and machine training and is advancing toward expert-level proficiency on the ADST system, ASM, and K&S wire bond machines.

🔧 Opportunities for Development & Growth

High overload in the lab builds prevented Haytam from contributing from his vast hands-on experience in discussions in engineering forums. He can also significantly contribute by pointing out product weaknesses and modifications not directly related to the process running on the machine under his responsibility.

In addition, especially during the least busy times, participation in meetings with Fabrinet was somewhat limited. A more active contribution, particularly by sharing feedback and insights based on hands-on laboratory experience, could have helped improve communication and alignment between the lab and the production line.

🎯 Future Deliverables & Goals

Haytam will be further integrated into strategic DC machine initiatives and will increase his involvement in DC-related meetings and discussions. This exposure will allow him to contribute more actively to decision-making and long-term planning. Additionally, he will serve as the Point of Contact for the Splinter product within the MTL BU in the process lab.

Haytam's professional development goals focus on deepening his expertise in controlled processes used in upcoming products, including Wolverine2, Havok, and Splinter. He will continue taking ownership of developing comprehensive Work Instructions (WIs) for future products and plans to increase his involvement in manual processes, such as the MA process.

From a technical leadership perspective, Haytam is expected to lead the MRSI acceptance process, including full hardware specification and coordination. In parallel, he will complete expert-level MRSI training and oversee and support the training of Samer, Michael, and Garold on the MRSI machine at L2.

He will complete training to achieve expert-level proficiency on the Ficontec machine and pursue advanced training at the Basi site for DC machine.

Furthermore, Haytam is expected to complete GPD training and Amicra training. As part of his continued technical development, he will also undergo training on the XYZ destructive testing machine.
